# Experimental US-English dictionary frontend for ONNX

CMUdict is licensed by Carnegie Mellon University (license in `data/CMUDICT_LICENSE`) and downloaded **only after a user clicks Download** in the TTS model manager. The exact revision is pinned, downloaded bytes are checked against an embedded SHA-256 digest and size, and the dictionary is saved in extension Cache Storage for offline inference. The large upstream dictionary is not bundled or committed to the PR. The source is `cmusphinx/cmudict` commit `74790861f652b15e4ac49015a90074ad62a27690` with blob `2c0411740cce3e2026a80b90b650d5f6a7258164`. All ARPABET→IPA logic is original extension code; no unlicensed phonemizer or external executable WASM is bundled.

Pronunciations are US English dictionary words only. Unsupported words, numbers and glyphs fail closed without remote speech fallback. Supported long sentences are split at normalized word boundaries into at most 510 phonemes per graph, with attached punctuation retained. The full request is validated before inference, and combined output retains the ten-minute PCM bound; requests remain limited to 20,000 text characters. Cancellation discards the active run's result and skips remaining chunks. Stress placement and prosody across chunk boundaries are not validated against Kokoro's training frontend. No claim of full English or multilingual coverage, perceptual quality, arbitrary long-form reading or POS-sensitive heteronyms is made.
